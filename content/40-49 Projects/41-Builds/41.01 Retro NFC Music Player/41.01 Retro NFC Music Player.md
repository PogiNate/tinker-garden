A self-contained, retro-styled Raspberry Pi music player: OLED now-playing display, physical transport buttons, built-in speakers, and NFC cards that start albums or mixtape playlists. Frontplate: single-part black/clear PETG print (manual filament swap ~1.5mm from the front) with backlit control label recesses; fuzzy skin on the rear face diffuses warm-white LED strips inside the cabinet.
