# Retro Player Project Retrospective

The Retro Player described in this project is effectively at version 1.0: all original goals are met, the final product performs as expected.
## Objective
The objective of the Retro Player is to create a device that has some of the qualities of a classic “hi-fi” system from the 1970’s-1990’s. These are the goals for the final project before choosing a technology stack or hardware or really anything else. Just the "dream" that this is meant to meet.

### Interface Requirements
- **Physically choosing music**: the Retro Player uses music “cards” that simulate the act of using a CD or cassette.
- **Physical buttons**: Instead of a phone-based interface, the final product should use physical buttons for:
    - Power
    - "Transport" (previous/play/pause/next)
    - Volume
- **Built-in speakers**: Just so the product can stand on its own if needed.
- **Monochrome activity screen**: To display current track and play state, etc. Green for preference. 
### Technical Requirements
A few technical requirements were top of mind before I started work on the project:
- **No Streaming Service**: All music must come from my library of _owned_ music. Absolutely no connection to Apple Music/Spotify/Tidal/Qobuz/Bandcamp/etc.
- **No actual physical media**: I'm not building a CD player or cassette deck or whatever; This should play digital audio.
