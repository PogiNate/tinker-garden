# Configs
Configs are, well, just that. Often when you set up a new tool you need to do some things to configure it. And then it works for a few years and when you[^1] have to re-configure it you have forgotten how. So I have taken to documenting them. 

# Workflows

Configs and workflows are projects I have undertaken to fix a specific issue that just needs a few things to happen. These are very common. Turning a file from one kind of file to another, or adding information to a database. These are usually things that have to happen once and then I forget how I did them before I have to do them again. These are slightly different from configs in that they often cross tools, and involve some minor scripting.

[^1]: And by "you" of course, I mean "I".
