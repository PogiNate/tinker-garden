# Welcome!

This is my repository of notes and reminders for the things I work on in my spare time. 

I have arranged this vault using the [[Johnny Decimal]] system, for now. I kind of like it, but we'll see if it is more _helpful_ or more of a _hinderance_ over time. 

## What Kindsa Things Are here?

I have a few different tinkering areas. I like working [[CircuitPython]] on microcontrollers, I like working in [[21.01 Ruby Language|Ruby]] or [[22.01 Python Language|Python]] on [[31.01 Linux|Linux]] machines. I am fond of shells like [[fish]]. 

I also do some basic electronics, involving a little soldering and whatnot. I try to make notes of things  I learn along the way so that I don't have to re-learn them along the way next time. I can tell you that I have learned to well gloves while soldering. Maybe other people can get away without gloves, I am not one of them.

I also do some 3D printing and I am learning more and more about how to optimize my workflows there as well. Have fun and poke around a bit!
