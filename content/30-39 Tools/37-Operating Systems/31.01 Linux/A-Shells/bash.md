# BASH: The Bourne-Again Shell

Ah, the puns. A sure sign of old-school Open Source Humor™. 

The _lingua franca_ of shell programming, even though it is showing its age.

The default shell on most Linux distros. Apple has just switched their default for MacOS[^1] from bash to [[zsh]] so that is suggesting that things are changing maybe? 

[^1]: I know, MacOS is _Unix_ not _Linux_. Can we just be cool?
